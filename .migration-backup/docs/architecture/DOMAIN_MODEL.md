# Domain Model

This document describes the primary OCHP business entities, relationships, ownership rules, lifecycle, and expansion points.

## Users

Purpose: represent authenticated application profiles linked to Supabase Auth users.

Relationships: users belong to a facility where applicable, have one canonical role, and appear as actors in audit logs, referral events, assignment history, and clinical notes.

Ownership: each user owns their account identity; administrators manage approval, role, facility assignment, and access state through secure IAM RPCs.

Lifecycle: pending, approved, rejected, suspended, deactivated.

Business rules: application access requires an approved active profile. Administrative changes require reasons and are audited.

Expansion: professional registration, clinician specialty, MFA status, county-level administrative scopes.

## Roles

Purpose: assign coarse-grained responsibility categories.

Canonical roles: `super_admin`, `facility_manager`, `facility_officer`, `chp`.

Relationships: roles map to permissions through `role_permissions`.

Business rules: legacy role names are normalized before permission checks.

Expansion: laboratory officer, radiology officer, pharmacist, county admin, read-only donor auditor.

## Permissions

Purpose: provide fine-grained authorization independent of UI pages.

Relationships: permissions are granted to roles and evaluated by `has_permission`.

Business rules: RPCs check permissions at execution time; frontend rendering is advisory only.

Expansion: module-specific permissions for lab, radiology, pharmacy, inpatient, and analytics.

## Facilities

Purpose: represent health facilities using OCHP.

Relationships: facilities own users, departments, CHPs, patients, referrals, notifications, and audit scope.

Ownership: facility-scoped records are visible only to authorized users from the same facility unless super admin access applies.

Expansion: county/subcounty hierarchy, facility type, licensing, service catalog.

## Departments

Purpose: canonical facility departments for routing referrals.

Relationships: departments belong to facilities and can own referrals through department assignment.

Business rules: active departments are used for workflow routing; legacy free-text department fields remain for compatibility.

Expansion: department service hours, triage capacity, department queues.

## Patients

Purpose: represent referred people receiving care.

Relationships: patients are linked to referrals and facilities.

Ownership: patient records are facility-scoped and subject to PHI safeguards.

Business rules: active duplicate referrals are prevented for the same patient identifier where enforced by secure creation RPCs.

Expansion: longitudinal medical record, patient consent, household relationships, FHIR Patient mapping.

## Referrals

Purpose: central clinical workflow aggregate.

Relationships: referrals link patient, originating CHP, receiving facility, current owner, department, events, notes, assignments, SLA timers, notifications, and audits.

Ownership: current owner may be CHP, facility, department, or assigned user. Access validation combines creator/facility ownership and permissions.

Lifecycle: draft, submitted, accepted, received, assigned, triaged, in_consultation, treatment, outcome_recorded, completed, closed, cancelled, reopened, archived.

Business rules: status transitions are validated by the command engine. Frontend does not decide next status.

Expansion: FHIR ServiceRequest/Encounter mapping, referral attachments, clinical order modules.

## Referral Events

Purpose: immutable timeline of workflow, SLA, and notification events.

Relationships: events belong to referrals and optionally reference actor, owner, facility, department, previous/new status, and metadata.

Business rules: events are append-only from application perspective and generated by backend workflow functions.

Expansion: event correlation across integrations, external delivery receipts.

## Referral Assignments

Purpose: track ownership changes over time.

Relationships: assignment history belongs to referrals and references previous/new owner types, users, facilities, departments, actor, reason, and assignment type.

Business rules: assignment history is written when owner fields change or an assignment command executes.

Expansion: workload balancing, queue analytics, reassignment reasons taxonomy.

## Clinical Notes

Purpose: preserve clinical documentation by workflow stage.

Relationships: notes belong to referrals and reference author and previous/superseded notes.

Business rules: notes are versioned and should never overwrite previous clinical content.

Expansion: note templates, attachments, clinician signatures, FHIR Observation/ClinicalImpression mapping.

## SLA Timers

Purpose: track expected workflow timing and breaches.

Relationships: timers belong to referrals and workflow transitions.

Business rules: timers record target, warning, breach, completion, and pause/resume information.

Expansion: configurable SLA policies by facility, priority, service, and county.

## Notifications

Purpose: represent queued and visible operational notifications.

Relationships: notifications target users or facility-wide audiences.

Business rules: workflow commands queue notification events; delivery integrations may consume these later.

Expansion: SMS, WhatsApp, email, push delivery, escalation policies.

## Audit Logs

Purpose: accountability for security, IAM, and record changes.

Relationships: audit logs reference actor, facility, table name, record ID, and change payload.

Business rules: sensitive administrative and referral changes are audited by secure RPCs or triggers.

Expansion: tamper-evident storage, audit export packs, compliance dashboards.
